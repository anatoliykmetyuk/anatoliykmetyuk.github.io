Hi, I'm Anatolii.

I’m an engineer focused on structure — how systems, tools, and people fit together over time. I’m interested in how technical and human systems shape each other, and how to design them so they remain sustainable.

I actively contribute to open source projects including [sbt/sbt](https://github.com/sbt/sbt) and [scala/scala3](https://github.com/scala/scala3), working on ecosystem evolution and long-term maintainability. I also develop and maintain the [Context Layer](https://ctxlayer.dev), a structured approach and an engineering pattern for effective AI-assisted development.

This site is where I share technical ideas, experiments, and architectural thinking.

My CV is available here: [CV](/assets/files/anatolii-kmetiuk-cv.pdf).

If something resonates, feel free to reach out. You can find my email in the CV or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akmetiuk/).
